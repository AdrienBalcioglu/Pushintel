# Push Notifications Landscape — Competitive Analysis
**Author:** Adrien Balcioglu · Freelance PM · Eidos Labs · June 2026

> A PM-grade competitive analysis of the push notification market — Braze, Airship, OneSignal — and the strategic opportunity for an AI-first challenger.

---

## Why this analysis

Legacy push notification platforms were designed in an era when dashboards were the only interface for growth marketers. In 2026, that assumption no longer holds. LLMs have changed what "operating a tool" means — and no major player has rebuilt their product around this shift.

This repo documents the current competitive landscape and maps the whitespace for **Pushintel**, an AI-first push notification platform where growth marketers operate entirely via natural language.

## TL;DR

| Player | Core positioning | Target |
|---|---|---|
| **Braze** | Enterprise customer engagement hub | Large orgs with dedicated data & marketing ops teams |
| **Airship** | Mobile-first app expert | High-scale native apps (airlines, banking, retail) |
| **OneSignal** | Utility push infrastructure | Startups, indie devs, lean agencies |
| **Pushintel** | AI-first autonomous growth copilot | Growth marketers done with dashboard fatigue |

**The core insight:** Every major player is architecturally trapped by the era in which their product was built. None of them can natively ship a zero-UI, prompt-driven experience — it would require rebuilding from scratch.
